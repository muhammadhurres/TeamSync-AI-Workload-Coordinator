THE PROBLEM
COORDINATION FAILURE

Hackathon teams burn out from poor task distribution.

Team coordination breakdown, unequal workloads, and free-rider issues derail even the most talented hackathon teams. Without intelligent coordination, one teammate drowns in tasks while another is underutilized. Skill mismatches lead to rework, missed deadlines, and last-minute chaos — the enemy of any submission.

THE MATH
FAIRNESS ALGORITHM

Objective fairness, mathematically defined.

The Workload Fairness Score is a deterministic metric derived from statistical workload variance and skill alignment, computed on every schedule generation.

WORKLOAD FAIRNESS SCORE — FORMAL DEFINITION
WorkloadFairnessScore =
  100 × ( 1 − ( StdDev_Hours / Mean_Hours ) ) × SkillMatchFactor
StdDev_Hours
Standard deviation of hours assigned across all members. Lower = more balanced workload.
Mean_Hours
Average hours per member. Normalizes variance into a 0–1 coefficient of variation.
SkillMatchFactor
0.0–1.0 multiplier. Perfect skill alignment = 1.0; mismatches penalize the score.
Score Range
0 (chaotic, mismatched) → 100 (perfectly balanced and skill-aligned). >85 is healthy.
